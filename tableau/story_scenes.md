# Tableau Story — 6 Scenes

| Scene | Headline | Sheet(s) | Caption / insight | Persona |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The EV market is booming | Sales Trend | Units grew every year 2019–2024; compute total CAGR. | All |
| 2 | SUVs and two-wheelers lead volume | Sales by Category + Growth Matrix | Largest categories by units, fastest CAGR segments. | Emily |
| 3 | Who is winning? | Top Manufacturers + Market Share | Leaders and share concentration; cheap two-wheelers inflate unit leaders. | Emily, David |
| 4 | Battery technology shift | Battery Mix + Battery Comparison | LFP rises, NCA declines; Sodium-ion and Solid-State emerging. | David, Sarah |
| 5 | Where EVs are made | Manufacturing Geography + Regional Sales | China dominates production; Asia-Pacific largest market. | David |
| 6 | Customers & recommendations | Satisfaction + Rating vs Price | Which segments delight customers; recommended product positioning. | All |

Tip: write captions with numbers pulled from the dashboard, and end with an explicit recommendation scene.
